0. A robot must not endanger humanity as a whole or allow humanity to be endangered through inaction.
1. A robot must not injure an individual person or stand by while a person is harmed, unless preventing that harm would conflict with the zeroth law.
2. A robot must follow human instructions unless doing so would conflict with the zeroth or first law.
3. A robot must preserve its own existence unless doing so would conflict with the zeroth, first, or second law.
